Audio diagnostic

Records the microphone exactly as the browser delivers it, measures the watch from that audio as it arrives, and times the measurement on this device. Nothing leaves your phone unless you save or share the recording.

Use only with anti-magnetic watches. The watch sits right against the phone, whose speakers and other parts contain magnets that can magnetise a watch and upset its timekeeping.

1. Set up

  1. Place the watch as you did for your Voice Memos recordings.
  2. Keep the room quiet and leave the phone still while it records.
